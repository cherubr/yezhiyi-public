Yezhi Translate · Getting started

Read bilingual webpages and PDFs with your choice of translation service. Get the extension from the installation source provided by the maintainer; this site contains documentation, not extension source or installation files.

Set your language and service

Open settings and choose Interface settings → Interface language. English, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, Japanese, and Korean are supported; other browser interface languages fall back to English. This choice is separate from the source and target languages.

The same panel sets appearance: follow the system, stay light, or stay dark, with fifteen color themes, including Monochrome and Pure white, each with light and dark variants; Midnight Cyan with dark appearance is the default. Themes restyle extension panels, the popup, settings, the PDF tools, and the print toolbar only, so pages and documents keep their own colors. Display style separately controls bilingual or translation-only reading and the translated text's font, scale, color, weight, line height, and paragraph spacing.

Google Translate and MyMemory need no API credentials, subject to network and service limits. Baidu requires an App ID, key, and a successful connection test. AI services use your own API settings. Custom endpoints you add need their own address and model; a loopback endpoint requires no key but its server must be running. Configure rate and concurrency for each service in its details.

Translate a webpage

Click the floating button to translate or cancel. Hover or use the keyboard to open a vertical toolbar for translation controls, settings, PDFs, and feedback, in that order. It opens downward when space allows, upward otherwise, and scrolls in small viewports. Closing it restores any temporary position adjustment without changing the saved position. Drag the button to save a new position; it snaps to an edge only when you release it within 256px of one.

Point at a paragraph and press Shift to translate or restore it. The floating translation tooltip shows the actual enabled Chrome shortcut. Assign shortcuts at chrome://extensions/shortcuts. The suggested shortcut is Ctrl+Shift+Y on Windows/Linux, or Command+Shift+Y on macOS.

Automatic translation is off by default. Disabling automatic translation affects new page starts; cancel an active page to stop it. Requests pause while the page is hidden. Browser-internal pages, closed Shadow DOM, and restricted frames may not be accessible.

AI prompts and glossary

Choose from seven built-in translation styles, or copy one and adjust six preferences and advanced instructions. Set a global default or select a temporary template in the webpage panel or PDF toolbar. Templates and up to five historical versions stay on this device. Local previews send no requests; translation tests and comparisons use your configured AI service and may incur charges. Other translation services do not use prompt templates.

Both import areas offer sample JSON downloads and format guidance. Duplicate prompt templates can be skipped, copied, or replaced with history preserved. Glossary imports merge by source term and target language and need saving. Search and filter glossary entries by language or status, and edit individual entries. The limit is 500 custom terms and 256 KiB per glossary import.

Read, edit, and export PDFs

Open the PDF reader from floating tools, the popup, or settings. Use a local PDF or authorize a direct online source up to 50 MiB. For login-protected or redirected files, download first and open locally.

Starting translation sends eligible text to your chosen service. Parsing, OCR, editing, and export run on your device; page images are not uploaded to translation services. Manage downloaded OCR models and export fonts in advanced reader settings.

Translation review lets you inspect retained, incomplete, and fallback content. In-place revisions and independent editor drafts are stored separately. Automatic PDF export includes saved in-place revisions but excludes independent draft edits. Export page draft uses browser print preview; choose Save as PDF to keep editor changes. Export leaves the source file unchanged. Missing translations require your choice to translate them or retain the original.

Privacy and troubleshooting

Keys and endpoint settings stay on this device; ordinary preferences may sync through Chrome. Read the privacy explanation for recipients, storage, permissions, retention, and deletion options.

For connection issues, check the service settings, model access, account allowance, network, and site permission. Configured does not mean verified. Report reproducible issues in the public issue tracker with browser/version, interface language, translation direction, and selected service. Remove keys and private document content before sharing.
